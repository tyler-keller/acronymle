How to play

Each day there's one acronym. Work out what it stands for in 6 guesses.

You get the first letter and length of every word. Small grey words like of or and are filled in for you.

Fill in every word and press Enter. Each word is scored on its own:

  • A right letter, right spot
  • A in this word, wrong spot
  • A not in this word

Once you solve a word it stays locked in.

New puzzle at midnight. Want more? Practice mode.
